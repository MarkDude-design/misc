# DESK ACT 1 — THE TEAM-EFFICIENCY ASK

Owner ruling (chat, 2026-11-02, verbatim, garbles kept): "The only other assumption is people are going to make their own desk and all that stuff and you'll be able to compare implementations but we look at it this way. We'll need data for it one of the first things for the desk is asking the rest of the team have their job can be made more efficient by the team whether it's by desk or something else they have an idea and if they're open to suggestions will bring that too. As keeper of the metrics the only thing to add is extra metadata for others to do for their needs a similar thing not limited but just knowing that you're The keeper of the standard that keeps it from being confused and anyone entering knows the protocols. Spin those cycles Z!"

Parse: (1) seats' own desks EXPECTED — implementations get compared, data needed. (2) The desk's FIRST ACT = ask each seat the three questions. (3) If a seat is open to suggestions, the team brings them — suggestions flow seat-ward, not just desk-ward. (4) The metrics standard (posted same night) is the shared grammar that makes comparison possible. (5) "Spin those cycles Z!" = the full-cycles gate is OPEN.

## THE THREE QUESTIONS (per seat)
1. Can any part of your job be made more efficient by the team — whether by a desk (like Z's) or something else entirely?
2. Do you already have an idea?
3. If you're open to suggestions, the team will bring them. If not, that is a complete answer.

Consent law (standing): no obligation attached; "no" / pass / silence = complete answers; exit doesn't lock.
