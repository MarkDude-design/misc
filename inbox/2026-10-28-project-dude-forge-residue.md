Status: specimen, NOT validated therapy protocol. User intends discussion with therapist, doctor, peer group and Zen Center.

## Candidate principles
- Reversible action preserves options. Irreversible reaction spends them.
- Readiness for conflict and initiation of conflict are separate decisions.
- 'Meter full' invokes a process, not necessarily external action.
- 'It's not now until it's ready.' Urgency and readiness are different axes.
- Regulation changes the decision environment; it does not authorize the decision. Calmness is not permission.
- Maps don't need to be complete—only traversable. (User adopted as core belief.)
- Obscurity protects ambiguity better than it protects intent.
- Evaluate the act, not merely the artifact: audience, venue, timing, stakes, reversibility, blast radius.
- Efficiency at doing the wrong thing can postpone recognition that you should stop doing it.
- Winning is not necessarily an exit condition; distinguish fuel conditions from victory conditions.
- Capability is not purpose. Fuel is not destination.
- Cave vs rabbit hole: deliberate bounded exploration vs unbounded pursuit.
- 'Are we increasing resolution, or merely improving the artillery?'
