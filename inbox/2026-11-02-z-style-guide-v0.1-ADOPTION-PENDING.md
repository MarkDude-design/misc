## 1. Punctuation
- Oxford comma, adopted (the owner's own reference): red, white, and blue.
- COMMA BUMPS: doubled or stray commas from relay/paste artifacts are corrected in authored text at the proof pass. Every change gets a read-back before push (post-push refetch verify already standing; the proof pass catches errors before they ship instead of after). Desk terms, defined: push = any transfer to a remote; commits are local and pre-ship; the read-back rides the change, before it ships.
- Spaced em dash — like this — never a double hyphen.
- One space after a period. Straight quotes only in repo files (escaping safety — the backslash lesson is canon); no smart quotes.
- **FOSSIL EXCEPTION (v0.3):** the straight-quote rule governs authored prose only. Banked verbatim rides exactly as relayed — smart quotes, dictation garbles, and all (`wanr=t` stays `wanr=t`). Live receipt: consult run z-desk-41511687121-1, verdict "reject" — "changing `wanr=t` to `want` inside a verbatim quote makes it non-verbatim." Finder credit: man (live verdict) + the Round-2 consult findings that queued the exception.
- Ellipses live inside verbatim quotes and nowhere else.
