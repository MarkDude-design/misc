## 1. Punctuation
- Oxford comma, adopted (the owner's own reference): red, white, and blue.
- COMMA BUMPS: doubled or stray commas from relay/paste artifacts are corrected in authored text at the proof pass. Every push gets a read-back before commit (post-push refetch verify already standing; the proof pass catches errors before they ship instead of after).
- Spaced em dash — like this — never a double hyphen.
- One space after a period. Straight quotes only in repo files (escaping safety — the backslash lesson is canon); no smart quotes.
- Ellipses live inside verbatim quotes and nowhere else.

## 2. Capitalization
