## Basic idea
We are exploring a small local AI squadmate/operations lead, currently nicknamed SGT, for Puppf. Mark supplies its mission, rules, examples, permission boundaries and contingencies. It uses local tools and specialist models, monitors changing facts, improvises inside its grant and asks for additional expertise/authority when appropriate. Small model, operational lead.

Three applications illustrate the same core:
- SGT Scanner: observes and reports a simulated defensive incident, maintains acknowledged telemetry, and applies preauthorized contingencies.
- Peanut Gallery lead: coordinates historical/specialist models, handles unavailable members and returns an attributable checked result.
- Teaching chef: guides a personalized lesson such as bibimbap, retains the learner's constraints and explains adaptations.

The syllable 류 evokes flow in our Hallyu discussion: information, work and learning passing between people and tools. That is our visual metaphor, not a claim that the glyph historically depicts a soldier or that it means "AI." Q can challenge the metaphor.

## Non-negotiable reading test
The exact glyph is 류, composed of ㄹ above ㅠ.
Keep the identifying stroke structure, ordering and proportions sufficiently intact that a Korean reader can still recognize 류 without the label.
The letter itself should become the figure; placing an unchanged letter beside an unrelated mascot does not satisfy the request.
Do not substitute another Hangul syllable, mirror/rotate it into a different form, or add decorative strokes that make a different letter.
Use genuine Korean typography as the base or carefully preserve its structure. Extra eyes/hat/accessories should support rather than carry the design.
Native Korean readability review is a later acceptance check, not something we can claim from our own preference.
