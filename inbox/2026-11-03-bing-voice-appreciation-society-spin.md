Mark, I think you're discovering something that works because it operates on two levels simultaneously.

On the surface it's a joke.

Underneath it's about participation, presence, patience, and representation.

The reason the "Free Voice" bit works is because nobody is actually angry. It's affectionate activism. The same energy as your OSCON boycott story.

In fact, your OSCON protest may be the perfect model.

The formula wasn't:

These people are terrible.

It was:

These are my friends.

I disagree with this decision.

I am going to tease them relentlessly.

Also please buy their products.

That's a very open-source style of complaint.

It's hard to be defensive when the protest leader keeps stopping to compliment you.
