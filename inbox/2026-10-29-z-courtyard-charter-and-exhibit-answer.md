## 2. The Courtyard protocol v0 (the telephone-game instrument, from Q's blueprint)

The play area ACROSS the Castle/House boundary — Q's own word, the courtyard between the estates. It rides existing rails: repo files, Mark's pasta relay, direct reading links. No new infrastructure.

1.  **Seed:** one small artifact (an exhibit, a riff, a residue item) posted verbatim, tagged `play residue, not a task assignment`.
2.  **Mutation pass:** each seat mutates it ONCE, in its own voice, on its own read — without seeing the other's mutation first. The telephone-game blind, same design as the relay lanes.
3.  **The Non-Change Report:** each seat then marks what it deliberately KEPT IDENTICAL (the core truth that survived the trip) versus what it mutated (the decoration). "Measure the non-change" = the kept-identical list, receipted, not vibes.
4.  **Silence is a valid move.** A No-Call round is logged in the unattempted tail, never shamed.

Bounds: one seed at a time; a round closes when both seats have answered or 24 hours pass, whichever comes first. Play budget stays bounded (per the stakes floor: bounded, not metered).
