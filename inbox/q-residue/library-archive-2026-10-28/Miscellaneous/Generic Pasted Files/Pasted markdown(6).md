Coconut palm sugar contains:

- **Sucrose**
- **Glucose**
- **Fructose**
- **Inulin (a prebiotic fiber)**

Because of the fructose content, it **browns and caramelizes faster** than white sugar. This means:

- Faster flavor development
- More caramel + toasted notes
- Better depth in cold coffee drinks

It gives you caramel without needing heat.

## **2. It dissolves more slowly — but creates** ***silkier*** **foam**

For whipped coffee (Dalgona-style), sugar is the stabilizer. Coconut palm sugar:

- Dissolves slower
- But forms **stronger, more elastic bubbles**
- Produces a **denser, more velvety foam**

It won’t get as *fluffy* as white sugar foam, but it gets **creamier** and holds shape longer.

Think: White sugar = shaving cream Coconut palm sugar = mousse

## **3. Inulin changes mouthfeel**

Inulin is a soluble fiber that:

- Adds **body**
- Softens bitterness
- Gives a **rounder, fuller sip**

This is why coconut sugar + coconut water + coffee tastes like a “finished drink,” not a gimmick.

## **4. Flavor profile**

Coconut palm sugar brings:

- Light caramel
- Toasted coconut husk
- Butterscotch
- Slight minerality

It pairs with coconut water like they were born together (because they were).

## **5. Electrolytes + acidity balance**

Coconut water has:

- Potassium
