2. **Batch 1: Kombu + Mushrooms**

   - Add next batch of mushrooms.
   - Steep 10 minutes.
   - Remove and eat.

4. **Taste the liquid**   This is your umami base. It will get darker, rounder, and more viscous with each batch.
5. **Optional Reduction**
   - Reduce 10–70% depending on desired thickness.
   - Stop at nappe for glaze.
