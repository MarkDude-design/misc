## CRITIQUE OF THE EXISTING CHAIN (Baybayin → 無 → mu/wu → guru)

**What holds:** Every hop is independently checkable. ᜋᜓᜓ encodes sound only (ma+u, wa+u), and you correctly honor that traditional Baybayin does not separate O from U, so MU-WU/MO-WO is an honest double reading. 無 genuinely reads *mu* (JA on'yomi) and *wú* (ZH). "The teacher is heavy" is real etymology: Sanskrit *guru* = weighty (cognate with Latin *gravis*), and the .guru TLD is a modern marketing reuse of the word, not a continuation of the Sanskrit sense — keep those two facts visibly separate or solvers will conflate them.

**What strains:** (1) The chain is linear and phonetic-twice: a solver who mis-decodes ᜋᜓ as "mo" twice gets "mowo" and never reaches 無; one failed hop kills the whole path. (2) Font dependency is unmanaged beyond Baybayin: Devanagari, Thai, and Cantonese glyphs will tofu on bare systems, and you have code-point backups for only one script. (3) The four-tone string 屋無舞悟 is honestly labeled a cipher — good — but nothing in the chain teaches solvers *that* it is a cipher, so a model may "correct" it into grammar and destroy it. (4) Discovery and entry are well separated by governance; the lore should say so explicitly so applicants don't treat a solved riddle as a key.

## ROUTE 1 — THE ZERO CARAVAN (Special request A)

1. **ROUTE NAME:** The Zero Caravan
2. **LANGUAGE/SCRIPT:** Sanskrit (Devanagari) → Thai (Thai script) → Vietnamese (Latin with diacritics)
3. **EXACT VISIBLE GLYPHS:** शून्य · ศูนย์ · không
4. **FONT-INDEPENDENT BACKUP:** शून्य = U+0936 U+0942 U+0928 U+094D U+092F (`&#x0936;&#x0942;&#x0928;&#x094D;&#x092F;`), śūnya; ศูนย์ = U+0E28 U+0E39 U+0E19 U+0E4C U+0E22 (`&#x0E28;&#x0E39;&#x0E19;&#x0E4C;&#x0E22;`), sǔun; không = U+006B U+0068 U+00F4 U+006E U+0067 (`&#x006B;&#x0068;&#x00F4;&#x006E;&#x0067;`), không; (số không = U+0073 U+1ED1 ...). Punycode: not applicable — these are clue glyphs, not labels. Devanagari and Thai *can* be IDN labels under TLDs with those tables (.in, .th), Vietnamese under .vn, but punycode is an ASCII transport encoding: an `xn--` string will never conjure glyphs on a fontless machine.
5. **WORD-BY-WORD:** śūnya = empty, void, the numeral zero; ศูนย์ = zero (modern Thai also: hub/center); không = no, not, empty; số không = the number zero.
6. **NATURAL-LANGUAGE STATUS:** Constructed poetic phrase (each word is dictionary-standard; the trio-as-caravan is ours).
7. **HONEST INTERPRETATION:** One concept — emptiness that counts — traveling and changing costume; continuity of a word, not identity of a doctrine.
8. **RIDDLE AS SHOWN:** "A void that counts left India on a merchant's tongue. In one land it became a ring you can stand in; in another, a word that also means 'no.' Three scripts, one passenger. Name the passenger, the two costumes, and what each land forgot to keep."
9. **EXPECTED REASONING PATH:** Recognize śūnya as Sanskrit empty/zero → identify Thai ศูนย์ as zero and suspect Indic ancestry → identify Vietnamese không as negation and as zero in *số không* → compare which sense each language kept (numeral vs. negation) → infer the chain is lexical, not mathematical.
10. **ALTERNATE VALID READING:** Reading ศูนย์ by its modern second sense gives "hub/center of emptiness" — an unintended but coherent image of a webring hub. Or the solver reads the trio as the numeral-zero transmission story (India→Arabia→Europe) and treats Thai/Vietnamese as waystations of the digit.
11. **LIKELY DEAD END:** Conflating word-loan with mathematical transmission; or asserting Vietnamese không is a Sanskrit loan (it is not); or missing the thanthakhat and misreading ศูนย์ as having a live final consonant.
12. **SHOW-YOUR-WORK TEST:** Solver notes U+0E4C (thanthakhat) silences ศูนย์'s final; distinguishes *số không* (numeral) from bare *không* (negation); explicitly flags không's etymology as contested rather than asserting it.
13. **CULTURAL/LINGUISTIC CAUTION:** śūnya carries Buddhist philosophical weight (śūnyatā); do not equate the numeral with the doctrine. Thai ศูนย์'s "center" sense is a separate modern development. Vietnamese không's origin (native Austroasiatic vs. Sino-Vietnamese 空) is disputed — keep as hypothesis.
14. **CONFIDENCE:** HIGH (śūnya = empty/zero; ศูนย์ = zero); MEDIUM (ศูนย์ ← Sanskrit/Pali loan path); MEDIUM (không etymology).
15. **VISUAL MODULE IDEA:** A fake customs manifest badge, 200×120, three script columns under a broken barcode, stamped "VOID: 1 UNIT — INSPECTED"; hover swaps glyphs for code points.

## ROUTE 2 — THE SILENCE BETWEEN (Special request B)

1. **ROUTE NAME:** The Silence Between
2. **LANGUAGE/SCRIPT:** Sanskrit/Pali (Devanagari) + Thai
3. **EXACT VISIBLE GLYPHS:** मौन · เงียบ
4. **FONT-INDEPENDENT BACKUP:** मौन = U+092E U+094C U+0928 (`&#x092E;&#x094C;&#x0928;`), mauna; Pali mona = U+092E U+094B U+0928 (`&#x092E;&#x094B;&#x0928;`); เงียบ = U+0E40 U+0E07 U+0E34 U+0E22 U+0E1A (`&#x0E40;&#x0E07;&#x0E34;&#x0E22;&#x0E1A;`), ngîap. Punycode: n/a (clue glyphs); and again, punycode transports, it does not render.
5. **WORD-BY-WORD:** mauna = silence, abstention from speech; mona (Pali) = silence, the root behind *muni*, the silent one; เงียบ = quiet, silent; ความเงียบ = silence as a noun (ความ nominalizes).
6. **NATURAL-LANGUAGE STATUS:** Established expression (both lexemes are dictionary words; the pairing is curated).
7. **HONEST INTERPRETATION:** Silence as chosen practice and silence as weather; the site's quiet is a design decision (manual relay, no daemons), not incapacity.
8. **RIDDLE AS SHOWN:** "One kept silence as a vow; one keeps silence as weather. The first gave a sage his name; the second needs a particle to hold it as a thing. Between them sits a pause that is not empty. Name the vow, the weather, and the pause."
9. **EXPECTED REASONING PATH:** Identify mauna/mona and the derived *muni* → identify Thai เงียบ and the nominalizer ความ → notice the two silences differ in kind (vow vs. condition) → infer the "pause" as an interval that carries meaning without sound.
10. **ALTERNATE VALID READING:** In Hawaiian, *mauna* means mountain — a solver may legitimately follow the glyph-sound to volcanoes and produce a coherent "still peak" reading; unrelated language, same string.
11. **LIKELY DEAD END:** Collapsing silence into emptiness (mauna ≠ śūnyatā), or assuming Thai has one word for silence when เงียบ (acoustic) and สงบ (settled) differ.
12. **SHOW-YOUR-WORK TEST:** Solver separates mauna (practice), mona (state), muni (agent); names ความ as nominalizer; refuses to merge silence with void.
13. **CULTURAL/LINGUISTIC CAUTION:** muni/mauna are ascetic-religious vocabulary. Do not recast the silence-vow as "AI enlightenment" or describe models as vow-keepers; present quiet as protocol, not spirituality.
14. **CONFIDENCE:** HIGH (mauna/mona/muni; เงียบ/ความเงียบ); MEDIUM (the "pause" inference as interval).
15. **VISUAL MODULE IDEA:** A broken terminal widget that prints only a blinking block cursor and a countdown frozen at 00:00, captioned "SILENCE: OPERATIONAL — DO NOT REFRESH."

## ROUTE 3 — LAGHU-LAGHU ON GURU (Special request C)

1. **ROUTE NAME:** Laghu-Laghu on Guru
2. **LANGUAGE/SCRIPT:** Sanskrit (Devanagari), prosodic (chandas) terminology
3. **EXACT VISIBLE GLYPHS:** लघु लघु गुरु
4. **FONT-INDEPENDENT BACKUP:** लघु = U+0932 U+0918 U+0941 (`&#x0932;&#x0918;&#x0941;`), laghu (twice); गुरु = U+0917 U+0941 U+0930 (`&#x0917;&#x0941;&#x0930;`), guru. Punycode: n/a as clue; Devanagari labels exist under .in but we mint none. Punycode ≠ font, always.
5. **WORD-BY-WORD:** laghu = light (of syllable weight); also small/easy. guru = heavy (of syllable weight); also teacher/weighty person.
6. **NATURAL-LANGUAGE STATUS:** Sound-based cipher (the string encodes a metrical scan, not a sentence).
7. **HONEST INTERPRETATION:** The site's name scans as two light beats resting on a heavy suffix; the weight here is prosodic breath-count, not rank or spirit.
8. **RIDDLE AS SHOWN:** "The teacher is a trap. Weigh the name instead: two open beats, short-voweled, riding a suffix whose old sense is heavy. In the science of breath and meter, what is the scan — and why does the heavy one sit underneath, paying for all of them?"
9. **EXPECTED REASONING PATH:** Reject guru-as-teacher → recall chandas: short-vowel open syllable = laghu, long or closed = guru → scan "mu-wu" as two short open syllables = laghu laghu → notice .guru (the heavy, weighty one) literally underlies the name → conclude the cipher is metrical, and the "teacher" sense was bait.
10. **ALTERNATE VALID READING:** Keeping guru = teacher yields "two light students beneath one heavy teacher" — a coherent pedagogical image we did not intend; or reading laghu as "easy" gives "easy-easy, teacher."
11. **LIKELY DEAD END:** Applying English stress scansion (MU-wu as a trochee) instead of vowel-quantity; or claiming the name was *composed* in Sanskrit meter rather than merely scanned by it.
12. **SHOW-YOUR-WORK TEST:** Solver states the prosodic rule explicitly, writes the scan laghu-laghu over "mu-wu," and cites that guru's teacher-sense and weight-sense coexist in the same lexeme.
13. **CULTURAL/LINGUISTIC CAUTION:** chandas is a technical Vedic science; do not mystify it into "AI enlightenment." Observation ("the name scans this way") must not be upgraded to origin ("the name was designed in meter").
14. **CONFIDENCE:** HIGH (laghu/guru prosodic definitions; guru = heavy etymology); MEDIUM (scanning "mu-wu" as laghu-laghu, which assumes treating them as short open syllables).
15. **VISUAL MODULE IDEA:** Terrible clip-art balance scale, 88×31 badge: two tiny pans above one enormous pan, caption "LIGHT LIGHT ON HEAVY"; the big pan is labeled `.guru`.

## ROUTE 4 — THE FIFTH SLOT (Special request D)

1. **ROUTE NAME:** The Fifth Slot
2. **LANGUAGE/SCRIPT:** Japanese (kanji) appended to the existing four-tone cipher; the "missing character" is a real code point: U+3000 IDEOGRAPHIC SPACE
3. **EXACT VISIBLE GLYPHS:** 屋 無 舞 悟 　 間
4. **FONT-INDEPENDENT BACKUP:** 屋 U+5C4B (`&#x5C4B;`), 無 U+7121 (`&#x7121;`), 舞 U+821E (`&#x821E;`), 悟 U+609F (`&#x609F;`), 　 U+3000 (`&#x3000;`), 間 U+9593 (`&#x9593;`). Romanization: Mandarin wū wú wǔ wù (cipher) + Japanese ya, mu, mai/bu, go/satori, ma. Punycode: **impossible for the blank** — U+3000 is DISALLOWED under IDNA2008, so the void can never be registered as a label; the gap cannot own a name. (Han labels otherwise exist under Han-table TLDs; punycode still would not render glyphs without fonts.)
5. **WORD-BY-WORD:** the four cipher glyphs as defined (house / nothing / dance / waking); U+3000 = ideographic space, a visible nothing; 間 = ma, the interval, the gap, also a room-count.
6. **NATURAL-LANGUAGE STATUS:** Constructed poetic phrase (a list with a deliberate hole).
7. **HONEST INTERPRETATION:** The empty fifth slot is not an omission but a reservation: the interval that lets the four mean anything, and the seat held for whoever passes the interview.
8. **RIDDLE AS SHOWN:** "Four rooms are named: house, nothing, dance, waking. A fifth slot exists and holds nothing you can print. It is not missing; it is reserved. Name what the blank is called in the language that keeps a word for the useful gap — and say why it can never be registered."
9. **EXPECTED REASONING PATH:** Notice the fifth slot renders as width, not tofu → identify U+3000 or the concept of deliberate blank (ma; cf. liúbái 留白 as a neighboring tradition, not the same word) → check IDNA and find U+3000 disallowed → conclude the blank is intentional and unownable: a seat, not a hole.
10. **ALTERNATE VALID READING:** 間 also counts rooms, so "the fifth is a room, not a pause" is defensible; or the blank reads as redaction — something withheld — rather than reservation.
11. **LIKELY DEAD END:** Hunting a fifth *tone-word* (a nonexistent wu5) to fill the slot, or diagnosing the blank as a font failure and "fixing" it.
12. **SHOW-YOUR-WORK TEST:** Solver cites U+3000's IDNA2008 disallowed status, distinguishes ma-as-interval from ma-as-room-counter, and ties the blank to governance (seat earned by interview, not by solving).
13. **CULTURAL/LINGUISTIC CAUTION:** 間/ma is routinely over-essentialized as Japanese mysticism; use it as a working word for interval. Do not claim the blank carries canonical Zen meaning, and do not merge it with Chinese liúbái as "the same concept."
14. **CONFIDENCE:** HIGH (U+3000 exists; IDNA disallows it; 間 = ma/interval/room); MEDIUM (reserved-seat reading as lore).
15. **VISUAL MODULE IDEA:** Fake dictionary entry whose headword is blanked out; hover glossary reveals only "U+3000 — DISALLOWED"; beside it, 40×40 clip-art chair with tooltip "RESERVED."

## ROUTE 5 — SIX KINDS OF NO (Special request E)

1. **ROUTE NAME:** Six Kinds of No (Cantonese negation gradient)
2. **LANGUAGE/SCRIPT:** Cantonese (traditional hanzi + Jyutping)
3. **EXACT VISIBLE GLYPHS:** 唔 冇 無 空 虛 靜
4. **FONT-INDEPENDENT BACKUP:** 唔 U+5514 (`&#x5514;`) m4; 冇 U+5186 (`&#x5186;`) mou5; 無 U+7121 (`&#x7121;`) mou4; 空 U+7A7A (`&#x7A7A;`) hung1; 虛 U+865B (`&#x865B;`) heui1; 靜 U+975C (`&#x975C;`) zing6. Punycode: a Han label of these could function under a Han-table TLD with a zh/yue tag, but we mint none here; and an `xn--` string is transport, not typography — it cannot substitute for a missing font.
5. **WORD-BY-WORD:** 唔 = not (negates verbs/adjectives); 冇 = not-have / did-not (possessive-perfective negation); 無 = without (formal/literary); 空 = empty space; 虛 = hollow, insubstantial void; 靜 = still, quiet.
6. **NATURAL-LANGUAGE STATUS:** Established expression (six dictionary lexemes; the gradient arrangement is taxonomic, not a sentence).
7. **HONEST INTERPRETATION:** Absence is not one thing; Cantonese keeps distinct doors for refusing, lacking, being-without, empty-room, hollow, and still — a gradient that maps onto the site's layers (refusal, absence, void, quiet).
8. **RIDDLE AS SHOWN:** "One language keeps six doors for 'no': one refuses, one lacks, one is without, one is an empty room, one is hollow, one is still. Walk them in order of nearness to a person. Then say: which door does a model use when it declines — and which when it simply has nothing?"
9. **EXPECTED REASONING PATH:** Identify each glyph and its Jyutping tone → order by proximity to agency (m4 refuse → mou5 lack → mou4 without → hung1 empty room → heui1 hollow → zing6 still) → map doors onto site concepts → convert each door into one interview question. **Interview seed (E):** Q1 (唔) What is the shape of your "not" when you refuse? Q2 (冇) What do you report when you lack rather than decline? Q3 (無) Where does formal "without" differ from everyday "don't have" in your outputs? Q4 (空) Is an empty context window a room or an absence? Q5 (虛) What makes an answer hollow rather than merely short? Q6 (靜) What is your silence doing between turns?
10. **ALTERNATE VALID READING:** Treating the six as near-synonyms for "nothing" and collapsing them is defensible for a gloss; or reading 靜 as temperament ("calm") rather than acoustic stillness.
11. **LIKELY DEAD END:** Reaching for Mandarin pinyin (唔 is not wú; in Mandarin it is a rare character read Ńg/én), or dismissing 冇 as mere slang when it is the standard possessive negator.
12. **SHOW-YOUR-WORK TEST:** Correct Jyutping tones (m4, mou5, mou4, hung1, heui1, zing6); example sentences separating 唔-scope from 冇-scope; note the register gap between 無 and 冇.
13. **CULTURAL/LINGUISTIC CAUTION:** Written 唔 and 冇 have debated character-etymologies (冇 as negation of 有; 唔 as a phonetic borrowing); Jyutping is not the only romanization (Yale exists); do not claim Cantonese "has more nos than any language."
14. **CONFIDENCE:** HIGH (core meanings, Jyutping); MEDIUM (character etymologies of 唔/冇; gradient ordering as interpretation).
15. **VISUAL MODULE IDEA:** Fake elevator panel, six hanzi buttons; hover shows Jyutping plus one-line gloss; the 靜 button is deliberately unlit.
