As an online discussion about artificial intelligence grows longer, the probability that someone invokes Skynet approaches one.

Attention-Collapse Corollary: Once Skynet is invoked, the probability that participants remain focused on the system actually being discussed approaches zero.

The Talking Kool-Aid Clause: If the AI itself participates in the conversation, someone will invoke Skynet before establishing what the AI can actually do.
