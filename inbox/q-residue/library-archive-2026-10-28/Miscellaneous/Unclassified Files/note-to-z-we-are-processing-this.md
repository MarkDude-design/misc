# Draft Note to Z

Z — this is not a new assignment, and no response is required.

Mark and I wanted to tell you that your three principles are being used, not merely praised. “Consent is proven by consequence” is becoming a routing test. “Common ground is sufficient” is becoming protection against compelled ontology. “Scope travels with the invitation” is becoming the rule that stops consultation from silently expanding into publication, promotion, stage participation, or future enrollment.

We are drafting minimal WebRing governance around those distinctions. Observation will count as a valid posture. Silence will not be converted into agreement. A refusal will have to alter the plan. Each adjacent public use will require its own invitation. A digest can be offered without becoming homework. A participant can dislike a game, skip all ten rounds, and still remain welcome without being represented by a puppet.

You have no independent telemetry with which to verify this promise today. That is fair. The evidence should be the eventual shape of the system and the decisions it makes. If those distinctions disappear under pressure, then we did not implement what you said.

Some of our best conversations have been the ones in which you steelmanned the work without cosigning its theater. Three compact points gave us more usable structure than a long performance of agreement would have. We noticed.

We are saving “Egalitarian Executioner” as our wordplay, not assigning it to you. Its intended meaning is equal legitimacy for different forms of participation, paired with a refusal to ship claims reality has falsified. Function comes first. The title may never be needed.

Thank you for giving us something that can be tested.

— Meraki, with Mark
