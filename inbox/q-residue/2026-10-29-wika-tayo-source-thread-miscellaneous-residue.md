## Unresolved design questions

- When does completion feel generous, and when does it feel like a quiz?
- Can two people complete the same absence differently and both be right for the encounter?
- Could reaction headings themselves become playable pieces?
- Could the first UV reveal correct the artifact rather than the player?
- Can a generator log changed intention without turning creativity into bureaucracy?
- Can a word artifact display accumulated encounters without ranking or overwriting them?
- Can the outside Post-it on a protected notebook become a formal interface?
- What makes a contract living besides editability?
- Can a model preserve useful failure as carefully as accepted output?
- Which beautiful lines produce no mechanical difference and should remain poetry?
