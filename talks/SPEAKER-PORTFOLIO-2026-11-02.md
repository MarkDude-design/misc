# Mark Terranova (MarkDude) — speaker portfolio

*Public-facing talk index, prepared November 2, 2026. CFP drafts and talk repositories are living materials; an entry here does not imply conference acceptance.*

Mark Terranova (MarkDude) is a longtime Linux and open-source community participant, maker, and speaker from California. His talks explore how tools change culture, and how people preserve authorship, accountability, and a way back when collaborating with AI. He brings a playful, hands-on approach to Linux, creative work, and community experiments.
